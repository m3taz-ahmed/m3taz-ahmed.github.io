Senior Architect Profile

I don't just write code — I engineer systems that refuse to fail. 15+ years of turning chaotic business logic into architectures that auditors trust and users never think about.

I started in 2009 building CMS solutions for small businesses. Today, I architect systems that handle 28,000+ bookings at peak load with 99.9% uptime — and I lead the Internal Audit digital transformation at one of Egypt's largest state-owned companies.
